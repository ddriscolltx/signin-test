Adding a passkey

ShopVisor sign-in test. Decision records and corrections need a passkey on your sign-in.

  1. Sign in to your ShopVisor test account's profile page with your emailed code.
  2. Add one passkey, on this device.
  3. ShopVisor then checks it, usually within one working day.
  4. From then on, the emailed code no longer opens your sign-in: use the passkey.
  5. Sign in once more in each assistant, from the connector you already have.
  6. Then add a second passkey on another device.

If both are lost, write to d@ShopVisor.ai.
